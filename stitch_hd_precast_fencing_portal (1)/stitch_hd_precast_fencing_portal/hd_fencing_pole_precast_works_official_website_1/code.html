Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions answered by Dharmpal Godbole and our civil engineering team.

The ideal standard distance recommended by civil engineers is 8 to 10 feet between poles. Spacing beyond 10 feet allows cattle or wild animals to bend the wire strands, whereas 8-10 feet provides maximum structural tension and rigidity. For 1 Acre (approx. 1,000 running feet), you require approximately 100 to 110 poles including corner angle struts.
Wild boars typically dig beneath fencing lines. We install high-tension dual-bottom strands pinned tightly to the ground with heavy stone/concrete footings, or we install 2 feet of buried chain link wire below the ground line. For Neelgai, we provide 8ft tall poles with 7 to 8 barbed strands or top angled extensions that prevent jumping.
We provide both! You can purchase wholesale poles, wire, and precast panels directly from our Pardi casting yard, OR you can take our complete Turnkey Contract where our team handles tractor digging, laser alignment, concrete anchor backfilling, wire tensioning, and delivery to your site.
We serve all of Nagpur District (Katol, Saoner, Umred, Hingna, Ramtek, Kalmeshwar, Kamthi, Narkhed) as well as neighbouring districts across Vidarbha: Wardha, Bhandara, Gondia, Amravati, Chandrapur, and Yavatmal. Our dedicated truck fleet delivers directly to your farm gate.
Our signature red and white painted cap is a hallmark of authentic HD Precast quality. The high-visibility safety red paint acts as an anti-encroachment deterrent and provides clear night visibility to approaching tractors, vehicles, and grazing animals, preventing accidental collision breakage.
