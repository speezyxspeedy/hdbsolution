## Brand & Style

The brand personality projects structural permanence, civil engineering rigor, and industrial accountability. Designed for institutional project heads, large-scale agricultural proprietors, real estate landbank developers, and industrial plant managers across Central India, the visual presence balances corporate reliability with infrastructural grit.

The design movement blends **Modern Industrial Utility** with **Precision Corporate Construction**. It draws inspiration from civil drafting blueprints, high-tensile material specifications, and architectural spec sheets:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, unshakeable, practical, and certified.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Structured grid systems, heavy-weight typography headers, crisp precision dividers, and vivid safety accents derived from physical highway and boundary markers.
- **Conversion Driver:** Fast quote accessibility, direct industrial-scale ordering, instantaneous WhatsApp civil engineering dispatch, and real-time fencing material calculators.

## Colors

The palette directly references precast concrete manufacturing environments, engineering drawings, and field boundary markers:

- **Primary (`#0A2540`):** Enterprise Contractor Blue. Signals longevity, corporate governance, and institutional engineering standards.
- **Secondary (`#DC2626`):** Boundary Pole Crimson. Replicates the high-visibility painted warning bands of standard boundary poles; utilized for primary action triggers and urgent estimates.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C`):** Vivid Safety Orange. Deployed for industrial badges, specification alerts, and construction warnings.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` to `#475569`):** Industrial Steel & Charcoal Slate. Dictates high-contrast data readouts, structural borders, and dense technical copy.
- **Canvases & Substrates:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) layered over Light Concrete Slate (`#F8FAFC` and `#F1F5F9`) for a clean, dust-free industrial finish.
- **Utility Accent (`#25D366`):** WhatsApp Brand Green, isolated strictly for floating dispatch contacts and instant technical quote actions.

## Typography

The pairing creates an architectural balance between structural power and legible specification tables:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its technical, engineered geometric cuts and angular joins that mirror modular precast concrete forms. Headings feature tight letter spacing to impart structural density.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides clean legibility across long industrial spec sheets, quote tables, and tender documentation. Open apertures allow high legibility under harsh sunlight on mobile devices on construction sites.
- **Labels & Spec Badges:** Formatted in uppercase or semi-bold variants with positive tracking to emulate stamped steel plates and cast mold serial codes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **12-column responsive modular grid** representing structural engineering cadences:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop`: 1280px+ (12 columns, 24px gutters, dynamic margins maxing at 1440px container).
  - `Tablet`: 768px - 1279px (8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px safe margins).
  - `Mobile`: 320px - 767px (4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px canvas padding).
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** All component paddings, card insets, and technical data grids are strictly clamped to a 4px/8px industrial baseline. Spacers never use arbitrary values; technical calculation cards use compact `space-md` paddings to keep parameters visible above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material separation over illusory atmospheric blur:

- **Surface Tiers:** Concrete-like layered flat strata. Base viewport is White (`#FFFFFF`), cards sit on Slate Tint (`#F8FAFC`), and structural parameter wells recess into Slate 100 (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines:** Every card, button container, and technical data row possesses a crisp 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0` on white, `#334155` on dark backgrounds).
- **Hard Ground Shadow:** When elevation is required for conversion prompts and active interactive inputs, elements use short, dense, non-diffused drops:
  - `elevation-sm`: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - `elevation-md`: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - `elevation-lg`: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08)`
- **Safety Strips:** Key enterprise cards feature a top 3px structural border in Crimson (`#DC2626`) or Contractor Blue (`#0A2540`) to reinforce high-visibility perimeter markers.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes structural stability and heavy precast units. High roundedness is intentionally avoided, as fluid curves contradict the geometry of precast boundary walls and reinforced concrete poles.

- Standard cards, interactive controls, inputs, and metric panels use crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Larger modular structural sections, pricing grids, and quote forms cap at `0.5rem` (8px).
- The only exception is status chips and technical badges, which adopt a machined industrial pill style (`9999px`) to maintain contrast against rectangular building block cards.
